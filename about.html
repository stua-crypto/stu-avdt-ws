About Stuart Abbott

My background

An examiner's eye, on your side

I'm Stuart Abbott. I run Abbott's Vocational Driver Training (AVDT) from Chudleigh, near Newton Abbot, and I train drivers and plant operators across Devon and Cornwall.

Before AVDT I was a DVSA driving examiner, testing both car drivers and lorry and bus drivers, including three years as a Vocational Examiner. I've seen first-hand what helps people pass and what trips them up, and that shapes how I teach. I've been training drivers for 30 years, and I've got over 30 years of driving experience behind me.

I also worked on the SAFED (Safe and Fuel Efficient Driving) programme as a trainer trainer, training the instructors who deliver it. Those economy skills are built into all my driver training, so your drivers learn to drive safely and use less fuel.

Today I deliver on-site forklift and telehandler training on customers' own equipment, LGV and PCV training days for driving schools, C1 training for trainee paramedics and ambulance technicians, and D1 minibus training.

Away from the road, I'm an RYA Powerboat Level 2 instructor and work as safety boat crew, providing water safety cover for triathlon swims and rowing events with Medical & Water Safety Ltd and Swim Safety Ltd. I've also volunteered as a rescue boat operator with York Rescue Boat, providing a water rescue service on the River Ouse on Friday and Saturday nights, and worked as a Flood Rescue Technician on the National Asset Register. As a PB2 instructor I can also arrange RYA-certificated courses through the Royal Torbay Yacht Club. More about my water safety cover.
