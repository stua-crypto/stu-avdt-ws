Fuel card report

Filled up on a company fuel card? Take a photo of the receipt, add the vehicle, place and mileage, then send a tidy report to your employer or the training school by WhatsApp or email.

Follow your company's fuel card rules first. This makes a report to go with the receipt; it doesn't replace the receipt. Your details stay on this phone and are only sent to the people you choose to send them to.

1. Your details

Filled in once, then remembered on this phone.

Never enter the full card number or PIN.

2. This fill-up

Receipt photo
Lay the receipt flat, in good light, with the whole slip in the picture. Up to 2 photos, e.g. a long receipt in two halves.

3. Send it

"Send report (PDF)" makes a PDF with your details and the receipt photo, and opens your phone's share menu so you can send it by WhatsApp or email. "Send as a message" sends the details as text only: attach the receipt photo yourself. Sending also adds the fill-up to your log below.

Your fuel log

Show

The log keeps the details of each fill-up on this phone, without the photos, so you can send a weekly summary or check it against your timesheet. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

Keep it on your phone

  • iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to home screen.
