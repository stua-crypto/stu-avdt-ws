Fuel and expenses report

Fuel on a company card, parking, tolls or a meal on the road? Scan the receipt, check the details, then send a tidy report to your employer or the training school by WhatsApp or email. Keep a weekly log and export it for the accounts.

Follow your company's fuel card and expenses rules first. This makes a report to go with the receipt; it doesn't replace the receipt. Your details stay on this phone and are only sent to the people you choose to send them to.

1. Your details

Filled in once, then remembered on this phone.

Never enter the full card number or PIN.

2. This receipt

What is it for?

At the pump: check the reg and type the mileage before you go in to pay, then show the screen at the kiosk. After paying: scan the receipt and the date, fuel station, litres, cost, card, reg and mileage fill in for you.

Scan the receipt and the date, place and amount fill in for you.

Receipt photo
The scanned receipt appears here. Lay the receipt flat, in good light, with the whole slip in the picture. Up to 2 photos, e.g. a long receipt in two halves.

3. Send it

"Send report (PDF)" makes a PDF with your details and the receipt photo, and opens your phone's share menu so you can send it by WhatsApp or email. "Send as a message" sends the details as text only: attach the receipt photo yourself. Sending also adds the fill-up to your log below.

Your log

Show

The log keeps the details of each receipt on this phone, without the photos, so you can send a weekly summary or check it against your timesheet or invoice. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Miles per gallon is worked out for each vehicle from the mileage at each fill-up, so it's only right if you fill the tank to full each time. Export spreadsheet makes a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, or imports into accounts software.
