FAQ

Common questions

Do we need to provide the telehandler, or do you bring one?

Training is carried out on your own machine, at your own site. A joint pre-use equipment check is done with your team before any candidate gets on it.

What if our operators have different experience levels?

That's fine — novice, experienced worker and conversion candidates can usually be planned into the same visit. Tell me the mix when you enquire and I'll confirm how that affects course length.

What happens if the weather or ground conditions aren't safe?

A dynamic risk assessment is carried out before and during every course. If conditions aren't safe, training pauses or stops until they are, or we reschedule.

How do we book?

Use the contact form or call 07807 395913 with your site details, the number of candidates, and a few dates that work.

Ready to book telehandler training for your team?

Get in touch with your site details and a suitable date — I'll take it from there.
