Digital walkaround check

Do your daily lorry walkaround check on your phone, for diesel or electric lorries. Tap each check, note any defects, then save or send your record.

Your operator's defect reporting system comes first. Use this if your operator accepts it, or as your own record. It follows DVSA's daily walkaround checks. Your answers stay on this phone and are never sent to AVDT.

1. Vehicle and driver

Lorry type

2. Checks

Tap OK or Defect for each one. For a defect, say what's wrong.

3. Your record

"Send or share" opens your phone's share menu, so you can send the record as a message by WhatsApp, text or email. "Save as PDF" makes a PDF copy you can save to Files or send. On a computer, the record is copied for you to paste and the PDF downloads.

Keep it on your phone

Add this check to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon.

  • iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to home screen.

You need a signal to open it. Once it's open, the checks work without signal and your answers are saved on the phone as you go.

Found a dangerous defect? Don't drive the vehicle. Report it straight away to the person in charge of defects. Defects found during the journey must be reported too: stop somewhere safe first.

Based on DVSA's daily HGV walkaround checks, with extra checks for electric lorries. Always follow your operator's procedures and the vehicle handbook. Prefer paper? Download the printable check sheet.
