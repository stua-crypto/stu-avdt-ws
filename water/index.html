Services

Water safety for your event

I provide on-water safety cover for event organisers, work as freelance safety boat crew alongside water safety providers, and train boat operators for rescue teams.

Triathlon swims

Safety boat cover for the swim leg, keeping watch on swimmers and ready to assist anyone in difficulty.

Open water swim events

Cover for sea, river and lake swims, from small club sessions to larger organised events.

Rowing events and regattas

Safety boat cover for rowing races and training, working to the event's safety plan.

Freelance safety boat crew

Extra qualified crew for water safety and event medical providers who need another pair of hands on the water.

Rescue teams

Powerboat training for rescue teams

I'm an RYA Powerboat Level 2 (PB2) instructor. I help rescue teams and volunteer organisations keep their boat operators up to date and bring new volunteers up to standard.

Refresher training for boat operators

Practical sessions to keep your existing operators current and confident, planned around the water and conditions your team works in.

Training new volunteers

Taking new volunteers from the basics of boat handling through to the skills they need before they crew or helm for your team.

Training is delivered in-house, shaped around your team's own procedures. If you need RYA certificates, I can arrange RYA courses through the Royal Torbay Yacht Club, where I'm a member, with certificates signed by the Principal.

I know how rescue teams train and work. I volunteered as a rescue boat operator with York Rescue Boat, providing a water rescue service on the River Ouse on Friday and Saturday nights. I'm an experienced Flood Rescue Technician, and was on the National Asset Register during my time there. Talk to me about your team's training.

Qualifications

Trained for the job

Water safety is about being prepared for the day something goes wrong. I'm an RYA Powerboat Level 2 instructor, and my qualifications cover safety boat handling, marine radio, first aid, survival at sea, firefighting and security awareness, including the full set of STCW basic safety training certificates.

This season I've worked as safety boat crew with Medical & Water Safety Ltd and Swim Safety Ltd, providing water safety cover at triathlon swims and rowing events. I've also completed the RYA Advanced Powerboat course.

I volunteered as a rescue boat operator with York Rescue Boat, covering the River Ouse on Friday and Saturday nights, and worked there as a Flood Rescue Technician on the National Asset Register, and I have experience as a swift water boat operator.

I'm Stuart Abbott, and my day job is driver and operator training. I've been training drivers for 30 years and I'm a former DVSA examiner. More about me.
