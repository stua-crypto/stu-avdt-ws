Course fees depend on candidate numbers and site travel. Get in touch for a tailored quote.

B1

Counterbalance forklift

Covers theory, practical operation and a formal test, with a certificate issued on completion. Training includes:

  • Pre-use inspection and daily checks
  • Stability, load centres and reading the truck's rated capacity plate
  • Picking up, carrying and setting down loads safely
  • Stacking and de-stacking at height
  • Travelling laden and unladen, including ramps and inclines
  • Working safely around pedestrians and in confined areas
  • Safe parking, shutdown, and refuelling or charging for your truck type

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need to provide the forklift?

Yes. Training is carried out on your own counterbalance truck at your own site, whether it's diesel, LPG or electric. A joint pre-use check is done with your team before any candidate gets on it.

Is forklift training a legal requirement?

Employers must make sure lift truck operators are adequately trained before they operate a truck. HSE's Approved Code of Practice L117 sets out what that training should cover, including basic training, specific job training and familiarisation. Every course I deliver follows it.

Can operators with different experience levels train on the same visit?

Usually, yes. Novice, experienced worker and conversion candidates can often be planned into the same visit. Tell me the mix when you enquire and I'll confirm how it affects course length.

Do you also train on telehandlers?

Yes. If your team uses telehandlers as well, see telehandler training. Both can often be covered in one booking.
