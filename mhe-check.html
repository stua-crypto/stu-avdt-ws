Daily pre-use check: forklifts and telehandlers

Do your daily pre-use check on your phone: counterbalance forklift, telehandler, reach truck or powered pallet truck, diesel, LPG or electric. Tap each check, note any defects, then save or send your record.

Your employer's procedures and the operator's handbook come first. Use this if your employer accepts it, or as your own record. It's a general guide to daily pre-use checks. Your answers stay on this phone and are never sent to AVDT.

2. Checks

Tap OK or Defect for each one. For a defect, say what's wrong and add up to 3 photos. Checks that don't always apply also have N/A.

3. Your record

Sending photos? "Send or share" sends the words only. To send your defect photos too, use Save as PDF and send the PDF, or take the photos again with the camera in WhatsApp after you send the message.

"Send or share" opens your phone's share menu, so you can send the record as a message by WhatsApp, text or email. "Save as PDF" makes a PDF copy, including any defect photos, that you can save to Files or send. On a computer, the record is copied for you to paste and the PDF downloads.

Keep it on your phone

Add this check to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon.

  • iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to home screen.

You need a signal to open it. Once it's open, the checks work without signal and your answers are saved on the phone as you go. Supervisors can print a QR sticker for each machine with the free MHE check sticker maker.

Found a dangerous defect? Don't use the machine. Take the key out, tell your supervisor or the person in charge of the equipment straight away, and make sure nobody else uses it until it has been made safe.
