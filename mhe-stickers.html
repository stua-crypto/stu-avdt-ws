Free tool for supervisors

MHE check sticker and card maker

Make a QR sticker or card for each forklift, telehandler, reach truck or pallet truck. Operators scan it on the machine or its key and the daily pre-use check opens with that machine already filled in.

1. Your machines

Example machines are filled in. Change them to yours, or paste a list below.

Paste a list instead

One machine per line: fleet number, then the type (counterbalance, telehandler, reach or pallet), then diesel, LPG or electric. For example: FLT 04, counterbalance, LPG. Anything left out is set to counterbalance and diesel.

2. Sticker options

Machine stickers for each machine
Key fob stickers for each machine (for the start key)
Machine cards (credit card size)

Use "A4 sheet to cut out" on a normal printer. "For a card printer" is only for printers that print plastic cards: on a normal printer each card fills a whole A4 page.

Machine stickers are 50 mm square, 15 to an A4 sheet: put one inside the cab or on the overhead guard where the operator sees it. Key fob stickers are 30 mm square, 40 to a sheet, and carry the same QR code and fleet number, so they stay with the machine's key. Each sticker has a thin grey cutting line.

3. Check and print

Scan a sticker below with your phone camera to test it before you print.

Machine card, front and back

Machine cards are 85.6 × 54 mm, the size of a bank card, with the QR code on the front and the check steps on the back. "A4 sheet to cut out" puts 8 cards on a page with the backs on the next page, lined up for double-sided printing. "For a card printer" makes one card per page, front then back. Choose double-sided in your card printer's settings. Laminate them, or slide them into a card holder on the machine's key or a lanyard.

Print at 100% size ("actual size"), not "fit to page". Print on plain paper and cut out, or on sticker label sheets. Do a test print on plain paper first and hold it against the label sheet to check the stickers line up. For machines used outdoors, weatherproof vinyl labels or laminating will make them last.

Everything happens in your browser. Your machine list is kept on this device so you can come back to it, and is never sent to AVDT. The stickers open the free daily pre-use check.
