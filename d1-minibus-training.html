FAQ

Common questions

Can I drive a minibus on my car licence?

It depends when you passed your car test. If it was before 1 January 1997, your licence will normally show D1 with code 101, which lets you drive a minibus with up to 16 passenger seats as long as it's not for hire or reward. If you passed on or after that date, you can only drive one on a car licence in limited volunteer situations; otherwise you need to pass the D1 test. The 2-minute check will tell you which applies to you.

What does code 101 on my licence mean?

Code 101 means "not for hire or reward". You can drive a minibus, but not where passengers pay to be carried or where you're paid to drive them commercially. To remove the restriction you need to pass the full D1 test.

What's involved in getting a full D1 licence?

You'll need a D4 medical completed by a doctor, then send it to DVLA with a D2 application to get provisional D1 entitlement. After that come the theory and hazard perception tests, practical training in a minibus, and the D1 driving test. You usually need to be 21 or over. I'll explain each step so nothing holds you up.

Do I need Driver CPC as well?

If you'll be driving a minibus as part of a paid job, you'll usually need Driver CPC too. Some types of driving are exempt, so tell me what the driving involves and I'll help you check.
