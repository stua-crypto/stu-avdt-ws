The service

An examiner's eye, on your candidates' side

I provide LGV practical driving training on behalf of driving schools across Devon and Cornwall — bringing candidates on as a school, and I do the training days that get them ready for their test.

C1 D1 C D C+E D+E

Trained by a former examiner

Having sat on the other side of the test as a DVSA Vocational Examiner for three years, I know exactly what's being assessed and where candidates typically lose marks. That goes straight into how I coach — direct, focused on what the test requires, and built to make the exam feel familiar rather than intimidating.

Built for training schools

I work as an extra pair of hands for schools that need an experienced LGV trainer for candidate training days, rather than taking on more than they can resource in-house. You keep the relationship with your candidate; I deliver the training day at a day rate.

Devon & Cornwall coverage

Based in Devon and available to travel across Devon and Cornwall to wherever your training vehicle and candidate are based.
