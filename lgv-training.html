C1 and D1 for individuals and businesses

Outgrown your 3.5 tonne van?

If your vans regularly run close to or over 3.5 tonnes, as many scaffolders, builders and other trades find, a C1 licence lets you drive vehicles up to 7.5 tonnes. I train individual drivers for C1, and D1 for minibuses, in your own vehicle.

C1

What you need to know

  • Passed your car test before 1 January 1997? Your licence probably already covers C1, up to 7.5 tonnes. Check the categories on your photocard first.
  • If not, you'll need a D4 medical and a D2 application for provisional entitlement, then the theory and hazard perception tests before your practical test.
  • Training in your own vehicle: it needs L-plates, and your insurance must cover a learner driver.
  • Test day: DVSA requires a C1 test lorry with a closed box body at least as wide and high as the cab, so a flatbed or dropside lorry can't be used for the test itself. I'll talk through the options with you.
  • Driver CPC: you may not need it if driving isn't the main part of your job. More about Driver CPC.
