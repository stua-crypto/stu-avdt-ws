FAQ

Common questions

Do we need to provide the truck, or do you bring one?

Training is carried out on your own equipment, at your own site — there's no fixed training centre and nothing for you to transport. A joint pre-use equipment check is done with your team before any candidate gets on the truck.

What's the difference between novice, experienced worker, and conversion training?

Novice is for candidates with no prior operating experience. Experienced worker is for those who've operated the truck type before but hold no formal certificate. Conversion is for certificated operators moving from one truck type or attachment to another. Course length varies between these — get in touch with your candidates' backgrounds and I'll confirm timings.

How many candidates can go on one course?

Group sizes are kept small so every candidate gets enough seat time and direct instruction. Tell me candidate numbers when you enquire and I'll confirm what that means for course length and days on site.

What happens if the weather or site conditions aren't safe on the day?

A dynamic risk assessment is carried out before and during every course. If conditions aren't safe — weather, ground, site activity — training pauses or stops until they are, or we reschedule.

How much does training cost?

Course fees depend on candidate numbers, course type, and travel to your site. Get in touch with your site location and candidate numbers and I'll come back with a tailored quote.

How do we book a course?

Use the contact form or call 07807 395913 with your site details, the equipment you need covered, and a few dates that work — I'll confirm availability from there.

Ready to book training for your team?

Get in touch with your site details and a suitable date — I'll take it from there.
