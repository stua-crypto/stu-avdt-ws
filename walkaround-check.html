Daily walkaround check

Do your daily vehicle check on your phone: lorry, bus, minibus or van, diesel or electric. Tap each check, note any defects, then save or send your record.

Your operator's defect reporting system comes first. Use this if your operator accepts it, or as your own record. It follows DVSA's daily walkaround checks. Your answers stay on this phone and are never sent to AVDT.

2. Checks

Tap OK or Defect for each one. For a defect, say what's wrong and add up to 3 photos. Checks that don't always apply, such as trailer checks, also have N/A.

3. Your record

Sending photos? "Send or share" sends the words only. To send your defect photos too, use Save as PDF and send the PDF, or take the photos again with the camera in WhatsApp after you send the message.

"Send or share" opens your phone's share menu, so you can send the record as a message by WhatsApp, text or email. "Save as PDF" makes a PDF copy, including any defect photos, that you can save to Files or send. On a computer, the record is copied for you to paste and the PDF downloads.

Keep it on your phone

Add this check to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon.

  • iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to home screen.

You need a signal to open it. Once it's open, the checks work without signal and your answers are saved on the phone as you go. Fleet managers can print a QR sticker for each vehicle with the free check sticker maker.

Found a dangerous defect? Don't drive the vehicle. Report it straight away to the person in charge of defects. Defects found during the journey must be reported too: stop somewhere safe first.

Based on DVSA's daily walkaround checks for lorries, buses and coaches and vans, with extra checks for electric vehicles. Always follow your operator's procedures and the vehicle handbook. Prefer paper? Download the printable lorry check sheet.
