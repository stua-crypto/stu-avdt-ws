Free tool for fleet managers

Check sticker and card maker

Make a QR sticker or card for each vehicle. Drivers scan it in the cab or on the keys and the daily walkaround check opens with that vehicle already filled in.

1. Your vehicles

Example vehicles are filled in. Change them to yours, or paste a list below.

Paste a list instead

One vehicle per line: reg, then lorry, bus or van, then diesel or electric. For example: WX21 ABC, lorry, electric. Anything left out is set to lorry and diesel.

2. Sticker options

Cab stickers for each vehicle
Key fob stickers for each vehicle (for the ignition keys)
Vehicle cards (credit card size)

Use "A4 sheet to cut out" on a normal printer. "For a card printer" is only for printers that print plastic cards: on a normal printer each card fills a whole A4 page.

Cab stickers are 50 mm square, 15 to an A4 sheet. Key fob stickers are 30 mm square, 40 to a sheet, and carry the same QR code and reg as the cab sticker, so they stay with the vehicle's keys. Each sticker has a thin grey cutting line.

3. Check and print

Scan a sticker below with your phone camera to test it before you print.

Vehicle card, front and back

Vehicle cards are 85.6 × 54 mm, the size of a bank card, with the QR code on the front and the check steps on the back. "A4 sheet to cut out" puts 8 cards on a page with the backs on the next page, lined up for double-sided printing. "For a card printer" makes one card per page, front then back. Choose double-sided in your card printer's settings. Laminate them, or slide them into a card holder on the vehicle's keys or a lanyard.

Print at 100% size ("actual size"), not "fit to page". Print on plain paper and cut out, or on sticker label sheets. Do a test print on plain paper first and hold it against the label sheet to check the stickers line up. For the cab, weatherproof vinyl labels or laminating will make them last.

Everything happens in your browser. Your vehicle list is kept on this device so you can come back to it, and is never sent to AVDT. The stickers open the free daily walkaround check.
