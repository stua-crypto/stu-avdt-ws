How far to trust it

Treat this as an estimate, not a promise. Published results for fuel-efficient driver training programmes have quoted average savings of about 10% for truck drivers, and some council road safety pages quote 10 to 15%. A New Zealand course reported 6.8%. That is why the slider starts at 5%.

Many of those figures were measured on or soon after the training day. Savings last only while drivers keep the habits, so compare your fuel use before and after.

Gallons are UK gallons (4.546 litres) and the CO₂ figure uses about 2.6 kg per litre of diesel. The 44 tonne artic starting figures, about 8 mpg and 73,000 miles a year, come from Road Haulage Association figures. Check them against your own fuel cards.
