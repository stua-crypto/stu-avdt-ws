Freelance instructors

Apply to work with AVDT

AVDT is building a UK-wide network of freelance instructors. If you hold current accreditation for the courses you teach, we'd like to hear from you.

Before you apply

  • Accredited and in date. Every course you want to deliver needs current accreditation. We can't consider an expired or lapsed registration. Renew it first, then apply.
  • Evidence. Upload your certificate or registration letter as PDF, JPG or PNG. Up to 5 files, 10 MB each.
  • Freelance. You would work as a self-employed instructor, not an employee, and have the right to work in the UK.
  • Insurance. We'll ask about your public liability and professional indemnity cover.
  • Driving licence. If you would teach LGV, PCV, C1 or D1, you need the licence entitlement for those categories.

Please don't upload

Your passport, a photo of your driving licence, your DBS certificate or any bank details. We don't need them to assess you.

For your licence, give us a DVLA check code instead. You can create one on GOV.UK, and it works for 21 days.
